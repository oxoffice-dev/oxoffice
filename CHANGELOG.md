# Changelog

All notable changes to oxoffice. Generated by git-cliff from conventional commits.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-23

### ✨ Highlights

- **PDF output closer to Word.** No more blank pages before a section break, endnotes below a final table, outline entries on their heading's page, words split across runs kept whole, over-long words broken where Word breaks them, column text no longer drawn over floating boxes, caption numbers read after their label, shading behind tabs, right-to-left tab stops and positional tabs placed as Word does ([#548](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/548), [#517](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/517), [#522](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/522), [#549](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/549), [#544](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/544), [#529](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/529), [#537](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/537), [#545](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/545), [#559](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/559)).
- **Equations everywhere.** Every OfficeMath construct is drawn in PDF and page images at the sizes Word gives it (within a point), and the RTF and ODT exports keep equations, which Word and LibreOffice read back ([#557](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/557), [#558](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/558), [#531](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/531), [#554](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/554), [#561](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/561)).
- **`compare` refuses less.** Block content controls (a Word table of contents included), endnotes, bookmarks both versions hold, simple fields in links or nested fields, links in paragraphs added or removed whole, and pictures reached through another relationship now compare, with redlines Word accepts and rejects cleanly ([#520](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/520), [#556](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/556), [#526](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/526), [#550](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/550), [#563](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/563), [#525](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/525), [#553](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/553), [#560](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/560)).
- **Links and fonts in PDF.** Cross-references and hyperlink fields are clickable and land on their bookmark, tagged links hold their text for screen readers, CFF system fonts are embedded correctly, and a fallback glyph no longer makes its line taller ([#540](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/540), [#528](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/528), [#542](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/542), [#523](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/523)).
- **Word editing.** Restart a style-numbered list, address a font record by its index, date controls with a time, localized Word files keep their own link and note styles, note marks and ruby read as Word reads them, RTF keeps paragraph styles and section layout (and Word-written RTF tables open again) ([#535](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/535), [#536](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/536), [#518](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/518), [#538](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/538), [#530](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/530), [#547](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/547), [#562](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/562), [#564](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/564)).
- **Behaviour changes to know.** Deck renders draw the characters their fonts lack (CJK) with a system font by default; pass `bundled_fonts_only` for machine-independent output (the Rust `*_with_diagnostics` render calls take the new option). `MathExpression` and `NoteNumberFormat` gained variants and are now `#[non_exhaustive]`; `NoteNumberFormat::Chicago` is new ([#539](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/539), [#557](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/557), [#546](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/546)).

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-23

### ✨ Highlights

- **Saves no longer fail on stray characters.** A character XML cannot carry (a bell, a form feed...) is refused or dropped where you set it, never at the save, in Rust, Python and the CLI; in Python, `XmlError` is now also a `ValueError` ([#504](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/504)).
- **Nothing lost on the round trip.** `&`, `<` and quotes reach every reader (Flat OPC export and ODP read work again with them), attributes are no longer escaped twice, on/off values and line-break `w:clear` survive edits, and a slide title survives ODP ([#503](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/503), [#488](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/488), [#482](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/482), [#496](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/496), [#505](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/505)).
- **Fields behave like Word.** `render_template` keeps a tag beside a field, REF reads what Word shows, a quoted operand joins text and nested fields, LibreOffice form fields are read, and bookmark names match in any case ([#481](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/481), [#489](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/489), [#497](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/497), [#480](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/480), [#502](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/502)).
- **Copy, cut and import keep documents whole.** Pasted and imported content keeps its ids unique and its bookmarks whole, headers and footers get the body's identities, and removed content leaves no orphan picture, chart or link behind ([#499](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/499), [#487](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/487), [#501](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/501), [#508](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/508)).
- **Faster slide reordering.** Moving or inserting a slide in Python no longer grows with the deck's shapes: about 50x faster on an 80-slide deck ([#494](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/494), [#479](https://github.com/oxoffice-dev/oxoffice/pull/479)).
- **Behaviour changes to know.** `add_bookmark` refuses a name that differs from an existing one only by case; a shared notes part is copied when one slide edits it; opening a file with such a character raises `XmlError`.
